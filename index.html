NAEP Achievement in Large Urban Districts by Socioeconomic Status

Trial Urban District Assessment (TUDA), 2024

Beginning with the 2024 assessments, the National Assessment of Educational Progress (NAEP) reports results by a socioeconomic status (SES) index built from student and school information. This site uses the NAEP Data Service API to show how achievement varies by SES within each of the 26 large urban districts that took part in the 2024 Trial Urban District Assessment.

NoteAt a glance (grade 8 mathematics)
  • In large cities, 52% of eighth-graders are in the Low SES group, compared with 33% in the nation’s public schools.
  • High SES eighth-graders in large cities scored 50 points higher in mathematics than their Low SES peers (national public: 49 points).
  • Across districts with reportable results, that gap ranges from 35 to 78 points.

Average scores by SES level

Each row is a district; dots show the average scale score of its Low, Middle, and High SES students. Districts are sorted by the Low SES average. National public and Large city are shown in bold for reference. Hover over a dot for the score and the share of the district’s students in that SES group.

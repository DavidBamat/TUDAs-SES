NAEP Achievement in Large Urban Districts by Socioeconomic Status

Trial Urban District Assessment (TUDA), 2024

Beginning with the 2024 assessments, the National Assessment of Educational Progress (NAEP) reports results by a socioeconomic status (SES) index built from student and school information. This site uses the NAEP Data Service API to show how achievement varies by SES within each of the 26 large urban districts that took part in the 2024 Trial Urban District Assessment.

NoteAt a glance (grade 8 mathematics)
  • In large cities, 44% of eighth-graders are in the Low SES group, compared with 28% in the nation’s public schools.
  • High SES eighth-graders in large cities scored 54 points higher in mathematics than their Low SES peers (national public: 53 points).
  • Across districts with reportable results, that gap ranges from 37 to 73 points.
  • On the 9-point (3-component) SES index scale, large-city eighth-graders with the highest index score (9) averaged 312 in mathematics, 68 points above those with the lowest score (0) (national public: 72 points). See SES Index Scores.

Results by SES Index Score

The Low, Middle, and High SES groups shown elsewhere on this site are built from an underlying SES index score. NAEP also reports results at each point of the index. This page uses the 3-component index (0 to 9) at both grades, so grade 8 results here use a different index than the 4-component (12-point) index behind the grade 8 SES groups elsewhere on this site. Higher scores mean higher SES. The point scale shows how achievement changes across the full SES range rather than across three broad groups.

Average scores by SES index score

Each cell is the average scale score of a district’s students at one SES index score. Darker cells mean higher scores. Districts are sorted by their overall average score, with the highest at the top. National public and Large city are shown in bold for reference. Blank cells are estimates NAEP did not report, which happens most often at the ends of the scale where few students are found. Hover over a cell to see the score and the share of the district’s students at that index score.

Distribution of students across the SES index

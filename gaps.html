SES Achievement Gaps

The gap between the average scores of High SES and Low SES students in each district. Larger bars mean a wider achievement gap. Gray bars are the National public and Large city comparison groups. Districts where either estimate was not reported are omitted.

Source: U.S. Department of Education, NCES, National Assessment of Educational Progress (NAEP), 2024 Mathematics and Reading Assessments, via the NAEP Data Service API.
